The cloud market doesn't wait
for anyone.

This week: AWS quietly updated its marketplace listing policies, Azure co-sell thresholds shifted, and GCP dropped a new partner acceleration program. Plus — a deep dive on your first 30 days.

What changed this week 5 updates
Marketplace New Tracker
AWS updated listing visibility rules — free trial offers now require explicit opt-out labeling
Amazon Web Services revised its Marketplace listing requirements for SaaS products offering free trials. Sellers must now display an explicit "opt-out before charge" label, or listings will be flagged for review during the next compliance cycle.

GCP launches Partner Acceleration Program — up to 20% margin boost for ISV partners
Google Cloud's new PAP offers incremental margin incentives for ISV partners meeting usage commitments. Early enrollment closes May 31.

Marketplace onboarding checklist updated — new tax ID verification step added
New sellers must now complete a tax ID verification before their first listing goes live. The step has been added to the standard onboarding flow.

AWS ACE pipeline now requires deal origin tagging for all new referrals
Amazon requires sellers to tag whether a deal originated from partner-led, AWS-led, or joint efforts. Untagged referrals submitted after June 15 will be returned.

Universal Guide · Chapter 1
Your first 30 days as a marketplace seller — what actually matters
Explorer Guide 8 min read

The first month in the marketplace is less about selling and more about setup. Most new sellers underestimate the time required for listing compliance, tax configuration, and buyer trust signals. This guide breaks down the 30-day window into three phases: configure, publish, and activate.

Phase one (days 1–10) is entirely administrative: tax ID, billing setup, and understanding the difference between a private offer and a public listing. Getting this wrong delays your first transaction by weeks.
